A soft spot for real assets

I don't have a favourite industry, although I have a soft spot for companies with real assets, particularly land and properties in locations that aren't easy to replicate.

I invest in real estate outside the stock market too, so I guess that explains part of my interest. I still remember looking through Veris Residential's apartment buildings and loving some of the properties, their views and amenities.

I can spend hours exploring buildings, land and developments on maps, looking at photos and trying to understand what makes each property interesting.

Unfortunately, I can't usually visit them in person, so I use whatever tools and information I can find to get a better idea of what they're actually like.

Liking the assets doesn't make it a good investment

There's much more to consider, and that's where I enjoy getting into the numbers.

  1. What it's worth

    I spend a lot of time on valuations, trying to work out what a business might reasonably be worth using assumptions I think are most likely to happen.

  2. What could go wrong

    I also want to understand what could go wrong.

  3. What it means for shareholders

    And how that value might eventually translate into returns for shareholders.

Sometimes I end up liking the business a lot more than the investment opportunity.
